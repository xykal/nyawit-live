# NYAWIT LIVE v6.0.0 — Monetization Status and Safety Boundaries

**Current v6.0.0 Status:** Gameplay rewards (`Rupiah Koin Kebun`, `Permata Sawit`, daily streak crates, and weekly PKS tournament tiers) are strictly virtual progression balances stored on the dedicated Cloudflare Durable Object SQLite database. There is no real-money cash-out, e-wallet withdrawal, Google Play Billing charge, or paid RNG gacha active in `v6.0.0`.

## Currency and Safety Boundary

- `Koin Game` (`Rupiah Kebun`) and `Permata Game` are virtual game progression balances earned through TBS harvesting, RAM PKS weighbridge deliveries, daily login streaks, and weekly plantation leaderboard milestones.
- No virtual balance, cosmetic item, leaderboard rank, or share card may be redeemed for DANA, GoPay, OVO, bank transfer, or cash value.
- No real-money prize contest is active. Any future sponsor-funded community tournament must be separately funded and undergo platform-policy and legal review before launch.

## Future Hybrid Roadmap (Post-v6.0.0)

1. Integrate Google Play Billing for optional direct cosmetic bundles with server-side purchase token validation and entitlement tracking.
2. Add optional consented rewarded ads with strict frequency caps and server-side reward verification (virtual progression rewards only).
3. Keep all randomized cosmetic crates virtual-ticket-only with published drop tables and server-side idempotency.
4. Keep any future sponsor-funded community events completely separate from RNG or paid virtual items, with published terms and eligibility rules.

See [Google Play Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738) and [Real-Money Gambling, Games, and Contests policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032).
